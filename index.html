About Us

One partner for your facility's equipment and engineering needs

Infibird Concepts Limited is a Ugandan company based in Bukoto, Kampala. We supply medical equipment, laboratory equipment and consumables, and ICT equipment. We also handle electrical, plumbing and air conditioning work.

Hospitals, clinics, laboratories, schools and offices come to us because we handle the whole job. We source from trusted brands, deliver, install and maintain, so you only deal with one team.

  • Trusted brands: Mindray, Olympus, Hettich, Memmert, Esco, Haier & more
  • Supply, installation, servicing and maintenance
  • Fast-moving lab consumables and rapid test kits in stock
